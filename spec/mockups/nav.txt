The navbar is shown at the top of each page. The sidebar is shown on the left.
For screen sizes of at least `lg`, the sidebar is always shown and the hamburger
button is hidden. For smaller screen sizes, the sidebar is hidden until you
toggle it open with the hamburger button.

When the sidebar is opened via the hamburger menu, it's rendered using the
native `showModal` method.
