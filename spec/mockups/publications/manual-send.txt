After clicking `Send` on the publication page:


  [breadcrumbs: <- Publication]

  # Send newsletter

  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  | [ ] My post                                                      |
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  | [ ] Another post                                                 |
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  | [x] Example post                                                 |
  --------------------------------------------------------------------

  [btn primary: Preview]


`Preview` opens a modal:


  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  | <Subject>                                                        |
  |------------------------------------------------------------------|
  | <Email content>                                                  |
  |------------------------------------------------------------------|
  |                                [btn: Cancel] [btn primary: Send] |
  --------------------------------------------------------------------


After clicking `Send`, you're redirected back to the publication page.
If any publication settings or post fields happen to change between clicking
`Preview` and clicking `Send`, still just use whatever's in the database. That
edge case doesn't matter.

Previews are rendered with DOMPurify.
