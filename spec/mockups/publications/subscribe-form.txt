defaults to "Enter your email" and can be customized in publication settings.

The hosted page fills the viewport, with one third of the free vertical space
above the form and two thirds below. The embedded form takes the full iframe
width and resizes its height to its content without scrollbars. The HTML page
title is the publication title.
